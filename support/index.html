PlainPocket Support

Need help? Email bake.pious1c@icloud.com with your device model, OS version and the PlainPocket build number (Settings > About). We read every message.

Common questions

Do I need an account or a bank login? No. PlainPocket has no accounts and never asks for a bank login. You import files you download from your bank.

Which files can I import? CSV, OFX/QFX, QIF and PDF statements from many banks, plus exports from Copilot, Monarch and YNAB. The format is recognized automatically and duplicates are skipped.

How do I move to a new phone? Turn on iCloud sync in Settings, or create a database snapshot and export it. On the new phone, sign in to the same Apple Account.

How does the free trial work? The yearly plan starts with a free trial. We remind you two days before it ends if you allowed notifications. Cancel at least 24 hours before it ends in Settings > Apple Account > Subscriptions to pay nothing.

How do I restore my purchase? Tap "Restore purchases" on the subscription screen, signed in with the Apple Account that bought it.

What happens if my subscription lapses? You keep your data and can export it any time (CSV, JSON, Beancount). The app asks you to subscribe to keep using it.

A charge is in the wrong category. Open it in Review or Activity, pick the right category, and choose "Always for this merchant" to teach PlainPocket the rule.

How do I delete my data? Settings > Delete all data, or delete the app.
